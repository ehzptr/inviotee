## Design Specificity Verdict

**Category-generic luxury wedding template.** Cream ground (#F3E1CF), Cormorant Garamond italic display, gold/ink/seal palette, GSAP entrance animations, Phosphor icons — fully interchangeable with any premium wedding invitation. The only culturally distinct signals are the Indonesian formal register, the Q.S. Adz-Dzariyat verse, the music filename, and assumed Chinese-Java corner frames (appearance unverifiable from source alone).

**Detector findings** (5 warnings, 0 errors):
- `italic-serif-display` — Cormorant Garamond italic at 64px. False positive: editorial wedding register legitimately uses this.
- `cramped-padding` ×4 — Pill nav, flex containers, dialog. Partially intentional (mobile pill design); dialog genuinely lacks inset padding.

---

## Overall Impression

Well-engineered. GSAP animations, responsive canvas scaling, native dialogs, debounced swipe detection, clean tab state — all solid. The experience *works*. What it lacks is soul: Chinese-Java identity is implied rather than embodied. The emotional sequence reads like a product spec rather than a love story building toward celebration.

---

## What's Working

1. **Progress bar with per-slide timing** — 10-segment `#prog` with variable `DUR` array gives genuine position awareness. Strongest heuristic execution.
2. **Guest personalization via URL params** — `?to=Nama&group=VIP` drives substitutions everywhere. Clean architecture.
3. **Demo-mode transparency** — RSVP path explicitly says "Mode contoh: ucapan hanya tampil di halaman ini" when endpoint is empty.

## Priority Issues

### [P1] Gold-on-cream text fails WCAG AA contrast
`text-gold-ink` (#7D5A10 on #F3E1CF ≈ 3.8:1), `text-seal` (#9A2B1E ≈ 4.1:1), and `text-gold/70` all fall below 4.5:1 AA. The large decorative quote mark (96px, gold/70 opacity) on slide 1 and gold headings across slides 2–8 violate AA. Low-vision users cannot read couple names or the verse.
**Fix:** Raise gold luminance to ≥#8B6914 or darken cream to #EDE0CC. Make `text-gold/70` opaque.
**Command:** `/impeccable colorize`

### [P1] No aria-live regions for dynamic content
Countdown updates every second, guest name renders from URL params, comment counter increments — none announce to screen readers.
**Fix:** Add `aria-live="polite"` to `#countdown`, `#cCount`, `data-guest`. Use `aria-live="assertive"` for `#rsvpStatus`.
**Command:** `/impeccable adapt`

### [P1] Emotional sequence buries the love story
Slides 3–5 (Akad/Resepsi, Map) are pure logistics. Cerita kami arrives at slide 7 — after dates, times, and map. The peak-end rule demands the emotional high point late; instead it's mid-pack. RSVP (8) → Gifts (9) is culturally jarring in Indonesian context.
**Fix:** Reorder: Sampul → Ayat → Mempelai → Cerita → Galeri → Acara → Peta → Hadiah → RSVP → Penutup.
**Command:** `/impeccable layout`

### [P2] Missing focus-visible on nav chips and action buttons
Keyboard users tab through `.nav-chip`, `#btnMusic`, `#btnAuto`, `#btnQr` with no visible focus ring.
**Fix:** Add `focus-visible:outline-2 focus-visible:outline-offset-2 focus-visible:outline-gold-ink` to these selectors.
**Command:** `/impeccable audit`

### [P2] Gallery uses three duplicate images across 7 slots
Images 1/4/7 identical; 2/5 identical; 3/6 identical. Screams template placeholder.
**Fix:** Replace with unique imagery or label "Galeri contoh".
**Command:** `/impeccable polish`

## Persona Red Flags

**Alex (Power User):** No keyboard shortcuts (Ctrl+M for music, Space for autoplay). Arrow keys navigate but Enter doesn't activate the open button.

**Jordan (First-Timer):** Icon-only music/autoplay/QR buttons with no hover state explanation. "Ketuk untuk menggeser peta" shield vanishes quickly — Jordan may think map is broken. No SWIPE explanation.

**Sam (Accessibility):** No aria-live on countdown/guest name/comment counter. Missing focus-visible on nav chips. Gold/70% quote unreadable at low vision. No announcement on inert→interactive transition.

**Casey (Mobile):** 6–9s autoplay may outrun 3G map iframe load. `measure()` blocks first paint on throttled CPU. Gallery images lack `sizes` attribute.

**Riley (Stress Tester):** RSVP form has no draft preservation on refresh. `data-guest` selector silently overwrites if accidentally duplicated. Countdown `setInterval` stacks on page revisit without cleanup.

## Minor Observations

- `force3D: true` GSAP config creates stacking contexts that may interact unexpectedly with `z-[1]` corner frames on some browsers.
- `SplitText.create` runs regardless of `prefers-reduced-motion` — DOM nodes added unnecessarily.
- Music autoplay in restricted iframes silently fails with no fallback.
- 414px fixed canvas scaling trades touch target consistency for layout rigidity — worth documenting.
- Countdown ISO string has redundant timezone (`+07:00` + "WIB" label).

---

## Questions to Consider

1. **Do the corner frames actually look Chinese-Java, or are they generic filigree?** If ornamental without cultural specificity, the theme's identity collapses to a music filename and a verse.
2. **Why does the love story come after logistics?** The sequence feels designed by a project manager rather than an emotional architect.
3. **Is the 414px fixed canvas a hard constraint or design choice?** It creates an accessibility trade-off on extreme viewports.
