# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Plain static HTML/CSS/JS

## Users

Couples getting married who want a beautiful digital wedding invitation to share with guests via WhatsApp or other messaging platforms.

## Product Purpose

Create a stunning single-page wedding invitation website that wedding guests experience when they open a link. The page should feel personal, emotional, and memorable - making guests feel special and excited about attending the wedding.

## Positioning

A wedding invitation that goes beyond basic details - it tells the couple's love story, shows their photos, creates anticipation with a countdown, and makes RSVP effortless. Every detail is designed to make guests feel like they're part of something beautiful.

## Operating Context

- Guests receive a link via WhatsApp message
- They open it on their phone (primary) or desktop
- First impression lasts seconds - must be stunning immediately
- Mobile-first, but works on all screen sizes
- Pages snap into full-viewport sections for a cinematic scroll experience

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Single HTML file with embedded CSS and JS
- No external dependencies required (all assets inline or from CDNs)
- Must work offline after initial load
- SEO-friendly structure
- Fast loading on mobile networks
- Print-ready if needed

## Brand Commitments

None yet - this is a template/generic wedding site that will be customized per couple.

## Evidence on Hand

Reference site: TIWI & FATAH wedding invitation at envelope.id - shows full-featured wedding invitation with cover page, couple profiles, love story, countdown, event details, RSVP, gallery, and gifts sections.

## Product Principles

1. **Every scroll tells a story** - Sections flow naturally, building emotion as guests journey through the page
2. **First view must captivate** - The opening section stops thumbs and makes guests want to stay
3. **Mobile is primary** - Designed for phones first, desktop second
4. **Personal feels universal** - Template structure works for any couple while feeling deeply personal
